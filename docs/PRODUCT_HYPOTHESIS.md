# SYNGULAR — Mission 000 / Product Hypothesis

## Core hypothesis
Organizations and communities often inherit high-stakes territorial decisions through documents, historical measurements, models and plans that are not naturally represented as a persistent, spatial, temporal evidence layer.

Syngular tests whether a small system can turn those static claims into structured, auditable measurement missions — without requiring confidential corporate data.

## First proof
Find at least one material claim in a real environmental document that can be transformed from a static statement into temporally traceable territorial evidence.

## Deliberately unproven
- Who pays first.
- Whether communities are the best initial paying customer.
- Whether a proprietary sensor is required.
- Whether deviation detection creates enough economic value.
- Whether existing GIS / OSINT / monitoring tooling already solves most of this.
